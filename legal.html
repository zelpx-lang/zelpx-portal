POLICY 01

Terms of Service

Final terms should identify the legal entity operating ZELPX, define the platform services, account rules, acceptable use, suspension and termination, intellectual property, notices, dispute procedures, governing law, and amendment process.

POLICY 02

Customer Agreement

Final customer terms should explain accurate request details, permission to share relevant contact information with the contractor who receives a lead, communication expectations, independent contractor relationships, service estimates, work agreements, payments made outside or through the platform, cancellations, and complaint channels.

POLICY 03

Contractor Agreement

Final contractor terms should cover eligibility, identity and business information, required state and local licenses, insurance, truthful profile details, service-area and category limits, professional conduct, customer communications, lead use, privacy, prohibited conduct, and responsibility for the actual work and customer contract.

POLICY 04

Lead Purchase Policy

Final policy should define what information a lead contains, when a lead is considered delivered, what “exclusive” means and its limits, how prices are displayed, when a purchase is final, restrictions on copying or reselling customer information, duplicate or invalid lead review, and how a contractor can report a problem.

POLICY 05

Refund & Dispute Policy

Final policy should define eligible and ineligible refund reasons, evidence required, submission deadlines, review steps, decision authority, response targets, credits versus cash refunds, chargebacks, and how disputes are escalated. Avoid promising automatic refunds or denying statutory rights.

POLICY 06

Privacy Policy

Final privacy notice should accurately describe data collected from customers and contractors, purposes and legal bases where applicable, service providers (including hosting, authentication, and payment providers), disclosures, retention, security practices, user rights, cookies and analytics, children’s data, and contact details for privacy requests.

POLICY 07

Platform Role & Liability

Final language should accurately describe whether ZELPX operates as a lead marketplace, advertising/referral service, payment facilitator, or another role; clarify that contractors are independent businesses where legally appropriate; address screening and verification limits; and allocate responsibilities without attempting to waive non-waivable consumer protections or statutory duties.

POLICY 08

Payments & Commission

Final payment terms should disclose lead prices, any platform commission, payment processor fees where applicable, payout timing, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, tax reporting responsibilities, and the role of the payment provider. The wording must match the actual Stripe Connect configuration and money flow.

Questions or policy concerns?

Before launch, add a monitored business contact email and mailing address for legal notices, privacy requests, and dispute submissions.
