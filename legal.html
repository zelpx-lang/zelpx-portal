02 · Customer Agreement

1. Submitting a request

Submitting a home-service request is free for customers. You agree to provide truthful, current information about the project, requested service, property location, timing, and contact details; to have authority to request work at the property; and to complete the required phone verification. Do not submit another person's contact or property information without permission.

2. Review and publication

A request may be reviewed by ZELPX before publication. ZELPX may ask you to clarify details, verify your phone number or address, correct a category, or decline a request that appears incomplete, duplicate, unsafe, unlawful, or fraudulent. Submission does not guarantee publication, a response, a quote, or that a professional will accept the project.

3. Who sees your information

Before purchase, eligible professionals may see limited project information needed to decide whether the lead fits their services and service area. Your name, phone number, email, and full street address are intended to remain restricted. When one eligible professional successfully purchases the lead, the relevant contact and location information is disclosed to that purchasing professional so they can contact you about the project. The lead is not offered for a second purchase after it is sold.

4. Working with a professional

You choose whether to respond to a professional, request an estimate, check credentials, sign a contract, and proceed with work. You and the professional—not ZELPX—must agree on scope, materials, schedule, price, permits, payment method, cancellation terms, warranties, and completion. Review the professional's license and insurance directly with the relevant issuing authority or insurer where appropriate.

5. Safety and communications

Use reasonable care when arranging an on-site visit, do not share payment credentials or security codes, and report suspected fraud, unsafe conduct, harassment, or misuse of your contact information to ZELPX. ZELPX may assist with platform-related reports but does not supervise the project or guarantee resolution of disputes between you and the professional.

6. Customer fees

There is currently no ZELPX charge to submit a standard service request. If a paid customer feature is introduced, its price and terms will be shown before purchase and will require any legally required consent.

03 · Contractor Agreement

1. Independent business status

You use ZELPX as an independent business, not as an employee, agent, partner, joint venturer, or representative of ZELPX. You control whether to purchase a lead and whether to accept or perform a customer's project. You are responsible for your business, personnel, tools, transportation, estimates, contracts, taxes, permits, and work.

2. Accurate profile and verification

You must provide truthful legal and business names, contact details, service address, service area, selected services, work role, license information where required, and insurance information requested by the Platform. You authorize ZELPX to review submitted information and, where appropriate, check license status with public licensing sources or contact an issuing authority. ZELPX approval is a platform eligibility decision, not a government license, insurance certification, background check, or guarantee of competence.

3. Licensing and legal compliance

You must hold and maintain every contractor registration, trade license, specialty credential, permit, bond, and other authorization required for the specific work and location. Requirements can differ by state, county, city, trade, project value, and work role. You must not purchase or accept a lead for work you are not legally qualified to perform. You must promptly update ZELPX if a license expires, is suspended, is restricted, or changes. A ZELPX database result is informational and does not replace confirmation with the relevant state or local authority.

4. Insurance

You must accurately answer whether you currently carry insurance and provide requested insurer, policy-number, and expiration information where applicable. You must maintain insurance required by law, contract, or the nature of your work. A statement that insurance exists is not a certificate of coverage, and ZELPX does not guarantee that a policy is active or sufficient. Do not misrepresent coverage.

5. Lead access and customer information

You may view limited lead details only to evaluate whether to buy the lead. After a successful purchase, you receive exclusive access to the customer contact information for that specific project. You may use it only to respond to and perform or quote that project and related communications the customer reasonably expects. You may not resell, transfer, publish, scrape, bulk-export, or use the information for unrelated advertising, and must protect it against unauthorized access.

6. Professional conduct

Respond honestly and promptly; identify your business accurately; provide truthful estimates; follow consumer-protection, safety, building, licensing, privacy, and anti-discrimination laws; obtain required permits; and do not pressure, threaten, deceive, or harass customers. You are responsible for your employees and subcontractors and for all work performed under your business.

7. Account review and restrictions

ZELPX may request updated information, review profile changes, suspend lead purchases while credentials are pending or expired, and block accounts for fraud, safety risks, false information, unpaid amounts, or misuse of leads. You must not attempt to bypass a restriction by creating another account.

8. No guaranteed work or earnings

Purchasing a lead buys access to the lead information, not a contract, job award, customer response, minimum revenue, or guarantee that the customer will hire you. You decide whether the lead is commercially suitable based on the information displayed before purchase.

04 · Lead Purchase Policy

1. Lead listing and price

Each available lead displays the service category, project details made available for review, service location information, urgency where provided, and the price in U.S. dollars or equivalent account credits before purchase. ZELPX currently sets lead prices, including by service category and ZIP code. You are responsible for reviewing the displayed information and price before confirming a purchase.

2. One lead, one buyer

Each lead is intended for one purchasing contractor only. A purchase is successful when the Platform confirms the purchase and records it against the lead. The lead then changes to Sold and is removed from the available inventory. ZELPX will not knowingly sell the same lead to another contractor after a successful purchase. Technical retries or duplicate payment events must not create a second sale.

3. What is delivered

The purchased lead consists of the customer/project information recorded for that request, including the relevant customer name, phone, email, and full service address when those details are available and lawfully shareable. Delivery occurs when the Platform records the purchase and makes the lead's restricted contact details available to the purchasing contractor. ZELPX does not promise that the customer will answer, remain interested, accept a quote, or hire the contractor.

4. Eligibility and restrictions

Only signed-in, approved, phone-verified contractors with an active account, eligible service category and service area, and sufficient account credits may purchase leads. The purchase may be declined if the lead is no longer available, the category is inactive, the service location is outside the contractor's approved service area, or the account does not satisfy platform requirements.

5. Duplicate and invalid leads

ZELPX uses reasonable controls to identify duplicate or suspicious requests. The same customer/project should not be republished as a new saleable lead within five days after its sale. This control does not mean that every similar project is a duplicate; ZELPX may review the customer, address, service, timing, and project facts. Contractors must report a suspected duplicate or materially invalid lead under the Refund & Dispute Policy.

6. Permitted use; no resale

Lead information is confidential and may be used only for the purchased project. You may not resell or share it with another contractor, lead broker, marketing company, or unrelated third party. You may share only with personnel who need it to respond to the project and are bound by confidentiality and privacy duties. Delete or securely dispose of information when no longer needed, subject to lawful recordkeeping requirements.

05 · Refund & Dispute Policy

1. How to report a lead issue

Contact zelpx@hotmail.com within five calendar days after purchase. Include the purchase date, lead or purchase reference, a short description of the issue, and supporting evidence. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information. ZELPX may ask reasonable follow-up questions.

2. Issues eligible for review

  • The same customer and substantially same project was sold to the same contractor more than once, or the lead was sold to more than one contractor.
  • The contact information is materially incorrect, disconnected, or belongs to an unrelated person, after reasonable verification attempts.
  • The request is materially outside the displayed service category or location due to a ZELPX listing error.
  • The lead was already cancelled, withdrawn, or otherwise unavailable before the recorded purchase.
  • A verified technical error charged credits more than once for one lead.

3. Issues that ordinarily do not qualify

  • The customer does not answer, changes their mind, hires another provider, or does not accept your quote.
  • The project is smaller, larger, less profitable, or more difficult than expected where the listing accurately described the available information.
  • You decide the lead is not a good business fit after purchase, fail to respond promptly, or lack the required license, insurance, service area, or capacity.
  • You do not win the project or the customer chooses a different price or schedule.

4. Review and remedies

ZELPX will review the purchase record and available evidence and may contact the customer or check relevant platform records. If a claim is approved, the default remedy is restoration of the lead price as account credits to the contractor's ZELPX wallet. If the original payment method must be refunded by law or under the payment provider's rules, ZELPX will use the legally required method. Credits are not cash and are not transferable unless required by law or expressly stated by ZELPX.

5. Decisions and escalation

We aim to acknowledge a complete claim within five business days and provide a decision or status update within ten business days. Complex cases may take longer; we will explain why and provide an updated estimate. If you disagree with a decision, reply to the decision email with additional evidence for a secondary review. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable law or your right to contact a regulator or payment provider.

6. Chargebacks and account balances

Please contact ZELPX first about a billing error so we can investigate. We may temporarily restrict purchases while a payment dispute, suspected fraud, or chargeback is reviewed. We will not use this policy to prevent a good-faith dispute or to waive statutory payment rights.

06 · Privacy Policy

Depending on how you use ZELPX, we may collect account identifiers and profile information (name, email, phone number, role, and profile photo); customer project details (service category, description, urgency, ZIP code, and service address); contractor business and eligibility information (company name, selected services, service area, license numbers, license states and types, insurance status and policy details when provided); SMS verification status; messages and support communications; lead, wallet, purchase, and payment records; and technical, device, log, and security information.

We do not request customers' payment-card details to submit a standard service request. The current contractor profile flow supports a profile photo and collects license and insurance details as information fields; older or administrative records may contain references to license documents. Payment-card data is handled by the payment processor, not stored as full card numbers in the ZELPX application.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; verify phone numbers; validate service addresses; review contractor eligibility; publish and match service requests; sell and deliver exclusive leads; support customer-contractor communications; process account-credit payments and purchases; prevent fraud and duplicate requests; handle disputes, refunds, and legal requests; maintain records; and operate, secure, and improve the Platform.

3. Lead information sharing

Before a lead is purchased, access to customer contact information is restricted and only limited project details are shown to eligible contractors. When one contractor purchases the lead, ZELPX shares the relevant customer name, phone, email, and full service address with that purchasing contractor for the purpose of responding to the requested project. The contractor then acts as a separate business responsible for its own handling of that information. ZELPX does not authorize resale or unrelated marketing use.

We use service providers to operate the Platform. Current providers include Supabase for authentication, database, and related storage; Vercel for hosting and deployment; Stripe for payment processing; Twilio for SMS verification; and the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder for address matching. Provider involvement may change as the Platform develops. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal process, protect users or the Platform, investigate fraud or safety concerns, or support a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, lead delivery, payment and transaction records, security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information about certain processing, and to opt out of certain uses where applicable. We will verify requests and respond within the time required by law. We do not sell personal information for money. If the Platform engages in targeted advertising, data sharing, or other regulated processing in the future, this policy and required choice mechanisms must be updated before that activity begins.

7. Cookies, analytics, and communications

Cookies or similar technologies may be used for login sessions, security, preferences, and essential functionality. We may send service messages about account security, verification, purchases, and support. Marketing messages, if introduced, will provide legally required opt-out choices. 07 · Payments & Commission

1. Customer requests

Submitting a standard customer service request is free. A customer is not charged by ZELPX merely because a contractor purchases access to the lead. Any future paid customer service must show its price and terms before payment.

2. Contractor account credits

Contractors may add funds to their ZELPX account through the Stripe-hosted checkout flow. The wallet is credited only after ZELPX receives and verifies a successful payment event. The amount and currency are displayed before checkout. Contractors should review the amount before confirming payment. Account credits are used to purchase leads and are not a bank account, deposit account, stored-value card, or cash balance held for general withdrawal. Credits are non-transferable and are not redeemable for cash except where required by law or expressly stated in the final policy.

3. Lead purchase price

The lead price is displayed before purchase and is deducted from the contractor's available account credits when the purchase succeeds. A successful purchase is recorded in the contractor's transaction history and the lead is marked Sold. Taxes or other charges, if legally applicable, must be disclosed before they are charged.

4. Commission and platform fee — confirmation required

The current live technical flow identified in the ZELPX application is a prepaid contractor credit wallet: Stripe Checkout adds credits to the contractor's wallet, and the full displayed lead price is deducted from that wallet when the lead is purchased. The current implementation inspected for this draft does not show a Stripe Connect split payment or contractor payout from the lead sale.

Open business decision — do not publish as final until confirmed: A 20% commission was previously discussed, but the current wallet flow does not identify whether that 20% is included in the displayed lead price, charged separately, or intended as a share paid to another party. The final terms and payment implementation must use one consistent model. No separate 20% charge or payout is promised by this draft.

5. Refunds, failed payments, and disputes

If a payment fails, the wallet will not be credited. If a verified duplicate webhook or technical retry occurs, it should not create duplicate credits. Approved lead refunds are generally returned as account credits under the Refund & Dispute Policy unless a cash refund is required by law or payment-provider rules. Payment disputes and chargebacks may temporarily affect account access while investigated.

6. Taxes and records

Each contractor is responsible for determining and paying taxes arising from its business and projects, subject to applicable law. ZELPX may collect, report, or remit taxes where legally required. Contractors should retain invoices, wallet transactions, and purchase records for their own accounting.

7. Payment provider

Stripe processes card payments under Stripe's applicable terms and privacy policy. ZELPX does not store full payment-card numbers in the application. A contractor's relationship with the payment provider may be subject to separate provider terms.
