Questions

Good to know.

Is it free for homeowners?

Yes. Requesting a pro costs you nothing.

What happens after I send a request?

It goes to one local pro for your service and area, who follows up with you. You can sign in to see updates and continue the conversation.

Will several pros contact me?

No. Each request is sold to a single pro, so you hear from one pro instead of a crowd.

Does my job need a licensed pro?

It depends on your state and the type of work. Ask the pro for their license number and check it with your state’s licensing board.

How do pros pay?

Joining is free. A pro pays only to buy a request they want, and no one else can buy it.

Where does ZELPX work?

ZELPX is built for the United States. Enter your ZIP code at the top of the page to start a request.
