ZELPX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

ZELPX respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, account information, service address, ZIP code, project descriptions, uploaded photos, messages, contractor business information, license and insurance information, and technical information such as browser and device details.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to manage accounts, authenticate users, process service requests, match customers with contractors, operate our lead marketplace, process payments, communicate updates, prevent fraud, and improve our services.

4. Customer Requests and Contractor Access

Limited service request information may be shown to eligible contractors. Customer contact information is intended to remain private until a contractor purchases the corresponding lead. After purchase, relevant contact information may be shared with that contractor.

5. Information Sharing

We may share information with contractors who purchase relevant leads, and with service providers that support our platform, including hosting, authentication, database, and payment providers. We may also disclose information when required by law.

6. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party providers such as Stripe. Payment information is handled according to the applicable provider's privacy and security policies.

7. Google and Facebook Login

If you sign in using Google or Facebook, we may receive information you authorize those providers to share, such as your name, email address, and profile information. Your use of those services is also subject to their privacy policies.

8. Data Storage and Security

ZELPX uses third-party providers, including Supabase for authentication and database services and Vercel for website hosting. We take reasonable measures to protect personal information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts and transaction records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

11. Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your ZELPX account and associated personal information, contact us using the email address below. Some information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security and transaction purposes.

12. Cookies

We and our service providers may use cookies to maintain login sessions, support website functionality, improve security, and understand website usage.

13. Children's Privacy

ZELPX is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
