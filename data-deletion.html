ZELPX Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

How to Request Deletion of Your Data

ZELPX respects your privacy. Customers and contractors may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

To request deletion, send an email to: zelpx@hotmail.com

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • The email address associated with your ZELPX account
  • A request to delete your account and personal data

What Happens Next

We will review your request, verify your identity when necessary, and process eligible deletion requests.

Some information may need to be retained when required by law, for payment records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.
